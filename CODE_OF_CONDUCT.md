# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Triton Control community a
welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other people
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing discussions on what is best for the project and community
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning when mistakes occur

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- Disrupting discussions or repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews, and other communication related to Triton
Control. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in
public.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report violations privately to the project maintainers using a private contact
method listed on their GitHub profiles. Do not report sensitive conduct matters
in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably
possible. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must not participate in
handling the report. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of
Conduct. They may issue a warning, apply a temporary restriction, or permanently
ban a contributor when appropriate.

Anyone accused of a violation will be given an opportunity to respond when it
is safe and appropriate to do so. Retaliation against a person who reports a
concern in good faith is not permitted.
